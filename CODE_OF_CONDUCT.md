# Code of Conduct

## In short

Be decent. Assume the other person is acting in good faith. Criticize code,
not people.

## Standards

Examples of behavior that makes this project better:

- Being welcoming to people whose experience level, background, or native
  language differs from yours
- Accepting correction gracefully, and offering it kindly
- Focusing disagreement on the technical question at hand

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks, public or private
- Sexualized language or imagery, or unwelcome sexual attention
- Publishing others' private information without permission
- Sustained disruption of discussion

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces — issues, pull requests, discussions —
and when representing the project publicly.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior to **sadigaxund@gmail.com**. Reports are
handled confidentially. Maintainers may edit, remove, or reject
contributions that violate this document, and may ban a contributor
temporarily or permanently for behavior they judge inappropriate,
threatening, or harmful.

Maintainers who do not follow this document in good faith may face
consequences determined by other maintainers.
